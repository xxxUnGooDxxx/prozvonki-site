A ProZvonki safety feature

Help someone you love get out of a phone scam

Scammers create urgency, fear and confusion, then try to keep a person on the line. Protect Loved Ones notices an unusually long active call with an unknown number, warns the phone owner and alerts people they trust.

Why another safety layer matters

Knowing the rules is not always enough under pressure

Phone scammers use urgency and authority to keep people engaged. A timely warning and help from a trusted person can interrupt that pressure.

How Protect Loved Ones works

  1. Choose one to three trusted contacts and set warning times. A separate PIN protects these settings.
  2. Protection starts only during an active call with a number that is not in the phone's contacts. Calls with known contacts are not restricted.
  3. The phone owner is warned first. If the call continues, trusted contacts receive an SMS with the other party's number.
  4. A trusted person can step in by sending any SMS to the protected phone while the unknown call is active.
  5. When a critical safety event is detected, the current call ends and unknown incoming and outgoing calls are blocked until the unlock code is entered.

An extra safeguard when a one-time code arrives

In the RuStore build, if a message resembling an OTP arrives during an active call with an unknown number, ProZvonki ends the call, alerts trusted contacts and locks unknown calls.

The app does not listen to the conversation. It does not read or retain SMS history: only a newly received message is checked during the protected call.

The rule worth remembering

Never give a caller an OTP, password, PIN, security code or full payment-card details. End the call and contact the organisation through a number or website you found independently.

Who may benefit

  • older parents and grandparents;
  • teenagers beginning to use banking and online accounts;
  • anyone who finds it difficult to think clearly under pressure;
  • families that want an agreed emergency plan before a scam happens.

Set up protection together and with the phone owner's consent. This is a safety mechanism, not hidden monitoring.

Clear limits

  • call duration alone never ends a call;
  • the feature does not record or analyse speech;
  • it cannot recognise every scam or replace safe habits;
  • the full automatic SMS and OTP flow is designed for the RuStore build.
Availability: the feature is currently free; it may require a subscription in the future. Google Play capabilities differ because of SMS permission rules.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does ProZvonki listen to calls?

No. It does not record or analyse speech.

Can a trusted person stop a suspicious call?

During a protected call, a configured trusted contact can send any SMS to trigger the emergency flow.

Does the feature guarantee protection from scams?

No. No tool can guarantee 100% protection from every scam. Protect Loved Ones reduces risk and gives a family one more way to notice a dangerous call and step in. It complements — but does not replace — safe habits and official guidance.
