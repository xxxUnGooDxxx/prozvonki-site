FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is ProZvonki?

ProZvonki is an Android dialer for important calls: a call log with context, comments, tasks, CRM and reports.

Can I use ProZvonki as a regular dialer?

Yes. The site covers everyday scenarios: calls, contacts, favorites, speed dial, the call log and protection from unwanted calls.

How is ProZvonki different from the stock Android dialer?

Beyond calls and contacts, ProZvonki helps you see important missed calls, leave comments, create tasks, keep CRM cards and build reports.

Does ProZvonki have a CRM?

Yes. CRM features include a client card, history, tasks, comments, statuses and separation of personal and work contacts.

Can I create tasks after calls?

Yes. In the work scenario a call can become a task or a callback reminder with a due date and a comment.

Can I leave comments on calls?

Yes. Comments and importance stay next to the call in the log and on the call card.

How does ProZvonki help me remember to call back?

The app surfaces calls that need a response and lets you create a task or a callback reminder.

Are there call reports?

Yes. The site describes statistics and XLSX/CSV reports for calls, callbacks, unhandled cases and activity.

Where are call and CRM data stored?

The log, tags, blocklist, comments and CRM data are stored on your device. A backup can be saved to a `.prozbak` file and protected with a password.

How do the Google Play and RuStore editions differ?

Core dialer, log, CRM and report features are available in both editions. The RuStore edition additionally includes a beta feature that forwards incoming SMS to email.
