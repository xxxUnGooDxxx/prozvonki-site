About you

About you: discover what your calls say about you

ProZvonki does more than help with calls — it shows you something new about yourself. The “About you” section looks at your call history, finds your archetype, gives you 3 fresh facts every week and awards badges. It all happens right on your phone — your data stays with you, and you can share the result with friends.

Your call archetype

The app looks at how you communicate by phone and picks an archetype that describes your calling style. It is an easy way to see your everyday routine from a new angle — no quizzes or forms, just based on how you already call.

3 new facts every week

Every week “About you” shows three fresh facts about your calls. There is always a reason to open the app and learn something new — and sometimes unexpected — about yourself.

Badges and achievements

The app awards badges for different calling habits and achievements. It is a playful element: your calls become more visible and your progress more noticeable.

Fully local — your data stays with you

The analysis of your calls happens on the device. Your calls, numbers and contacts are never sent anywhere — the app only collects anonymized analytics about how features are used. You can learn new things about yourself without handing your personal data to someone else's cloud.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about “About you”

What is the “About you” section in ProZvonki?

It is a personal section that looks at your call history and shows you something new about yourself: it finds your archetype, gives you 3 new facts every week and awards badges. It is not about work — it is a fun way to learn curious details about yourself and share them with friends.

How is my call archetype determined?

The app analyzes anonymized characteristics of your call history right on the device — for example, how often and when you call and whom you call back — and picks an archetype from them. The analysis happens locally; your calls and contacts are never sent anywhere.

Are my calls and contacts sent anywhere?

No. The “About you” section works on the device: calls, numbers and contacts are not sent to third-party servers. The app only collects anonymized analytics about how features are used, without the content of your calls.

How often do new facts appear?

Every week the section shows 3 new facts about you, so there is always a reason to open the app and learn something else about yourself.

What are the badges for?

The app awards badges for different calling habits and achievements. It is a playful element that makes routine calls more visible and more fun.

Can I share the result with friends?

Yes. You can share your archetype, facts and badges with friends, while your personal call data stays on the device.
