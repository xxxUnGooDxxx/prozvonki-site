Reports and statistics

Call reports and statistics in numbers

ProZvonki helps you see call activity, callbacks, unhandled cases and problem spots. Reports can be exported as XLSX/CSV for further analysis.

Statistics for a day, week or month

Statistics help you understand the load and see how call activity changes over the selected period.

XLSX/CSV reports

The reports include a summary, the full call list, unhandled cases, callbacks and a day-by-day breakdown.

Unhandled cases and callbacks

The report shows where calls that need attention are left. It helps you keep track of missed calls and agreements to get back in touch.

Filters for work calls

SIM, call type, duration and contacts help you analyze the right segment instead of your entire call history at once.

Sending reports

The site describes both manual report sending and scheduled auto-send through your email.
