Android dialer

An Android dialer with useful features for work and life

ProZvonki works as a convenient everyday Android dialer, but adds what the stock app often lacks: a log with context, comments, quick callback, tasks and CRM features.

How ProZvonki differs from a regular dialer

A regular dialer helps you make or take a call. ProZvonki keeps the context: who called, what happened after the conversation, whether you need to call back and whether any calls are left unhandled.

Quick access to calls and contacts

The app has calls, contacts, favorites and speed dial 1–9. Frequent people stay closer, and the main in-call actions are where you expect them.

Call log with context

  • repeat calls and statuses in one place;
  • comments and importance next to the call;
  • search by number, name, status or tag;
  • quick callback from the log and the call card.

Protection from unwanted calls

A blocklist, unknown-number blocking and number rating help you decide whether to answer. This is useful for people who talk on the phone a lot and don't want to be distracted by unnecessary calls.
