Tasks and callbacks

Post-call tasks so you never miss the next step

After a conversation there's often an agreement: call back, clarify details, send information or close the case. ProZvonki helps turn a call into a clear task.

A task after a call

A call can be linked to a task so the next step doesn't get lost among other to-dos and incoming calls.

Due date and callback reminder

You can set a due date on a task. It helps you return to a client on time when you've agreed to talk later.

A comment on the conversation

A comment captures what was agreed and why the task appeared. The context stays next to the call and the client.

Task statuses

Statuses help you tell apart calls that need attention, are in progress, are scheduled or are already closed.

Control over unhandled calls

The ProZvonki work scenario is built around the chain: spot the missed call, schedule a callback, record the outcome and check the report.
