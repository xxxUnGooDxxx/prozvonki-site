Call transcription and conversation summary

ProZvonki can turn a call recording into text and make a short summary of it — right on the phone, without the internet. Neither audio nor text leaves the device. Below is how to enable it, what you can configure, and what affects quality.

Set up call recording first →

Before you start

  • Call recording must be enabled. Text and summary are made from the recording — set up recording first.
  • Everything runs on the device, offline. Nothing is sent to the network — neither audio nor text.
  • On first use, offline data is downloaded once (this uses traffic and phone storage).
  • Quality is beta. Errors are possible, especially with noise or when people talk at the same time.

What it gives you

Transcription turns a call recording into readable text — handy to re-read, to find an address, an order number or an agreement inside it. The summary is a short recap in a few sentences: what was discussed, what was clarified, and where you left off. A summary helps you recall the gist of a past conversation in a second before the next call.

Step 1. Turn on transcription

Open Settings → the “Data” section → “Call recording” and turn on the “Call transcription” toggle. On the first enable you need to accept a short consent — that processing happens on the device. After that, the rest of the text settings become available.

Step 2. Pick the recognition language

In the “Recognition language” item, choose the language the conversation is usually in. The set of available languages depends on the device. Picking the right language has a big effect on accuracy — if the language doesn't match the speech, the text comes out worse.

Step 3. Auto-transcription (optional)

The “Automatically” toggle transcribes a recording right after the call, with no manual start. The recognition data for the chosen language must already be downloaded. If auto-transcription is off, you can transcribe any recording manually with the “Transcribe” button in the call log.

Step 4. Turn on the conversation summary

Turn on the “Conversation summary” toggle — a “Summary” button appears in the transcript window. The summary is built from the transcript text: what was discussed, what was decided, key agreements, dates and amounts. A separate “Auto-summary” toggle makes a summary right after auto-transcription — note that on-device generation noticeably loads the CPU.

Step 5. Summary on the call screen

Two separate toggles control the hint on the call screens:

  • “Show on the incoming call screen” — the summary of your last conversation with this number appears on the incoming call screen.
  • “Show on the active call screen” — the same hint is visible during the call itself.

If you've talked to the number before, you'll see a short “Last time you discussed…” — a memory hook to recall the context at once. Both toggles are on by default and can be turned off independently.

What affects quality

The accuracy of the text and summary depends on a few things you can influence:

  • Clean recording audio. Noise, a quiet voice, poor audibility and speakerphone recording make the text worse.
  • Overlapping voices. When both parties talk at once, there are more errors.
  • The right recognition language. Choose the language the conversation is actually in.
  • Conversation length. Very long conversations may be shortened for the summary — shorter stays more accurate.
  • Phone performance. It affects speed: on weaker devices a long recording takes longer to process.
  • Summary quality depends on transcription quality. Inaccurate text in means a less accurate summary out.

Privacy

Transcription and the summary run fully on your phone. Audio and text never leave the device, nothing is sent to the cloud. The internet is needed only once — to download offline data on first use. After that it all works without a network.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about transcription and summary

Does my conversation go to the internet?

No. Transcription and the summary run fully on the phone, offline. Neither audio nor text leaves the device.

Is call recording required?

Yes. Text and summary are made from the recording, so set up call recording first.

What language will the text be in?

The chosen “recognition language”. The set of available languages depends on the device — pick the one the conversation is actually in.

Why is transcription slow?

Speed depends on the phone and the recording length. On weaker devices a long recording can take several minutes to transcribe.

Why are there errors in the text or summary?

Quality depends on clean audio and overlapping voices, and the feature is still in beta. The cleaner the recording, the more accurate the result.

Can I transcribe an old recording?

Yes. Any saved recording can be transcribed manually with the “Transcribe” button in the call log.

Do I need the internet?

No, except for the one-time download of offline data on first use. After that it all works without a network.
