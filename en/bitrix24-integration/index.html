ProZvonki integration with Bitrix24

ProZvonki links the Android dialer with your Bitrix24 portal: on a call the app finds the client by number, shows their name right on the incoming call screen, shows a pop-up card to the responsible person, logs the call in telephony and, when needed, creates a lead, a deal and a task. Everything works through an inbound webhook — with no server of your own and no intermediaries.

How to set it up →

Client lookup by number

At the moment of a call the app searches Bitrix24 for a contact, company or lead by phone number — in several formats (+7 and 8) so the match is found reliably. The call is instantly linked to the right client card.

Client name from Bitrix24 on the incoming call screen

A new capability: if the number isn't in the phone's contacts, the app finds the client in Bitrix24 and shows their name right on the full-screen incoming call screen — before you pick up. You immediately see who's calling, even if the contact exists only in the CRM. Enabled with a single toggle.

Pop-up call card

On an incoming or outgoing call, a card pops up in Bitrix24 for the responsible employee with the client's name and a link to the deal or contact. The manager doesn't have to look the client up by hand — the details are already there.

Auto-creation of a lead or deal

A call from an unknown number can automatically create a record in the CRM. The app respects the portal mode: in classic Bitrix24 a lead is created, in simple mode a deal with a contact. Nothing needs to be configured by hand.

Call logging in Bitrix24 telephony

Incoming and outgoing calls are logged in Bitrix24 telephony: direction, duration and status (answered, missed). Only the fact of the call is saved — the conversation audio is not recorded and is not sent anywhere. The call history builds up right in the client card, next to the deal or contact.

Flexible call scenarios

For each call type — incoming or outgoing, from a known or unknown number — you choose the action yourself: do nothing, just log the call or create a record in the CRM. No extra records pile up, and the ones you need aren't lost.

Tasks for calls

After a call the app can create a task in Bitrix24 itself — for example, to call back a missed call. Configured by scenario and outcome (answered or missed), with a due date, an observer and a title and text template. The task is linked to the found client.

No server of your own

The integration works through a Bitrix24 inbound webhook — the app talks to the portal directly. No intermediate server is needed, and the webhook secret is stored on the device in encrypted form. There's also an exclusion list: no records or tasks are created for service numbers.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the Bitrix24 integration

Do you need your own server for the Bitrix24 integration?

No. The integration works through a Bitrix24 inbound webhook — the app talks to your portal directly, so a separate server or intermediary isn't needed.

Which permissions does the webhook need?

For the full set of features tick crm, user, telephony and task: crm — client lookup and creating a lead or deal, user — the employee list, telephony — call logging (no audio) and the card, task — creating tasks for calls.

Are classic and simple CRM modes supported?

Yes. The app detects the portal mode automatically: in classic mode a lead is created, in simple mode a deal with a contact. The mode can also be set manually.

Does the app record conversation audio in Bitrix24?

No. The app only logs the fact of the call — direction, duration and status. The conversation audio is not recorded and is not sent to Bitrix24.

Can the client name from Bitrix24 be shown right on the call screen?

Yes. Enable “Show contact name from Bitrix24”: on an incoming call from a number that isn't in the phone's contacts the app finds the client in Bitrix24 and shows their name right on the full-screen incoming call screen — unlike the pop-up card, which is only visible in the web or desktop version of Bitrix24.
