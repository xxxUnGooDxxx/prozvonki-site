How to set up Protect Loved Ones

A complete guide for the protected phone owner and their trusted person: from choosing contacts to stopping a risky call and unlocking unknown calls.

Before you begin

  • the phone you want to protect;
  • one to three trusted numbers saved in its contacts;
  • one trusted person's phone;
  • Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Aegis or 2FAS on the trusted person's phone.
Important: keep the authenticator and backup codes with the trusted person, not only on the protected phone.

Setup in 13 steps

  1. Open the feature: Settings → Security → Protect Loved Ones.
  2. Read the introduction and tap Continue.
  3. Add 1–3 trusted people from contacts. Choose the number they actually use for SMS.
  4. Grant permissions for contacts and receiving and sending SMS. Message history is not read.
  5. Set the owner's warning. Default: after 1 minute, with sound, vibration and an on-screen caution.
  6. Set the first trusted-contact SMS. Default: after 2 minutes, including call duration and the unknown number.
  7. Set the second SMS. Default: after 3 minutes, together with a High Risk warning. Stages must remain in order.
  8. Review emergency protection. It is triggered by an during the call or any SMS from a trusted number.
  9. Choose extended protection. Optionally link an OTP and unknown call within 30 minutes before or after the conversation.
  10. Connect an on the trusted person's phone by scanning the QR code or entering the key, then verify a six-digit code.
  11. Save all 10 . Each works once; the complete list is not shown again after setup.
  12. Create a 4–8 digit settings . It protects trusted contacts, timings and feature deactivation.
  13. Review the summary and tap Enable protection.

The three risk stages

1 minute

Sound, vibration and a caution on the protected phone. This stage has its own adjustable time.

2 minutes

First SMS to trusted people with the unknown number. This stage has its own adjustable time.

3 minutes

Repeat SMS and a High Risk warning. This stage has its own adjustable time.

Each stage runs once per call. The time of each of the three stages is configured independently, and any stage can be disabled. Duration alone never ends a call.

How to stop a risky call

Trusted person's action

  1. Check that the call is still in progress.
  2. Send any ordinary SMS to the protected phone, such as “Call me back”.
  3. ProZvonki verifies the sender, ends the unknown call and locks unknown calls.

No command word is required. The SMS must arrive during the active unknown call.

When a one-time code arrives

An OTP received during an unknown call triggers the same emergency flow: the call ends, trusted people are alerted and unknown incoming and outgoing calls are locked. An ordinary SMS without a numeric code should not trigger it.

How to unlock unknown calls

  1. Tap the “Unknown calls are blocked” banner.
  2. Contact the trusted person.
  3. Ask for the current six-digit authenticator code or use one backup code.
  4. Enter it and tap Unlock.

Emergency numbers, trusted people and contacts saved before protection triggered remain available. The lock has no timer and stays on until a valid code is entered.

Troubleshooting

An alert SMS was not sent

The call lock remains active. Use Notify trusted contacts again and check mobile service, the SIM plan, SMS permission and the saved trusted number.

A six-digit code does not work

Choose the ProZvonki entry, enter exactly six digits, try the newly refreshed code and enable automatic date and time on both phones. Repeated failures temporarily pause new attempts.

The trusted phone is lost

Use a previously saved backup code, then set up a new authenticator and backup-code set.

Frequently asked questions

Does protection work for incoming and outgoing calls?

Yes, for unknown numbers. Timing starts after the other party answers; ringing and time on hold are excluded.

Will a call end after three minutes?

No. Timed stages only warn. Emergency stopping needs an OTP or a trusted person's SMS during the active unknown call.

Can stages be configured separately?

Yes. Each can be enabled, disabled and timed separately, but their order must be preserved.

Can the settings PIN unlock calls?

No. Unlocking needs the trusted person's authenticator code or a backup code.

Does the authenticator need internet?

No. Codes are generated locally.

Are contacts or SMS uploaded?

No. Contacts, the call log and message content are not sent to an external server.

Can the feature make a mistake?

Yes. No automated tool provides 100% protection. It reduces risk and gives trusted people another way to intervene, but does not replace safe habits.

Is the feature free?

It is currently being tested for free. Terms may change later. Standard carrier SMS charges may apply.

Quick checklists

For the phone owner

  • never share OTPs, card details, passwords or security answers;
  • pause and verify the caller when a warning appears;
  • ask the trusted person for a code to unlock calls.

For the trusted person

  • keep the authenticator and backup codes with you;
  • contact your loved one quickly after an alert;
  • send any SMS if the active call appears dangerous;
  • never share the QR code, secret key or backup codes.
