Themes and appearance

Themes and appearance: dark theme, AMOLED and a clean screen

ProZvonki can be customized to your taste: a dark theme and AMOLED, 10 ready-made themes, accent colors, list or card view and a clean screen mode that removes extra log elements. The dialer looks exactly the way you want.

Dark theme and AMOLED

Turn on a dark theme or choose an interface scheme: light, dark or AMOLED with a deep black background. AMOLED saves battery on OLED screens and is easier on the eyes in the dark.

10 ready-made themes and accents

Choose from 10 ready-made themes — Classic, Graphite, Blue Office, Teal, Contrast, Warm, Purple and others. You can set an accent color and enable colored call arrows: green for connected, red for missed, yellow for handled.

List or card view

The log and lists can be shown as cards or as a compact list where items are separated by a thin line. The list takes up less space and fits more calls on the screen.

Clean screen

  • hide the quick filters on the main screen;
  • full-screen log without the top header;
  • remove the HD, VoLTE, Wi-Fi and SIM number icons;
  • hide the info button and internet number lookup.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about appearance

Does the dialer have a dark theme and AMOLED?

Yes. You can turn on a dark theme and choose an interface scheme — light, dark or AMOLED with a deep black background that saves battery on OLED screens.

How many ready-made themes are there?

There are 10 ready-made themes — for example Classic, Graphite, Blue Office, Teal, Contrast, Warm, Purple and others. Each sets a combination of accent, missed-call color and warnings.

Can I choose an accent color?

Yes. Besides the ready-made themes you can set an accent color, enable colored call arrows and keep the “Call” and “Hang up” buttons in the standard green and red.

Can I show the log as a list instead of cards?

Yes. There is a “List view” mode: items are separated by a thin line instead of cards across the whole app, making the log more compact.

What is the clean screen?

It is a set of options that hide extra elements of the main log: quick filters, the top header and the HD, VoLTE, Wi-Fi, SIM number and info icons. The call screen becomes minimal.
