Incoming call screen

Incoming call screen: flash, edge light and easy answering

In ProZvonki you can customize how an incoming call looks and behaves: a full-screen view or a heads-up notification, a camera flash, a soft edge light frame, answering by swipe or buttons, and even auto-answer when you raise the phone to your ear. Calls become more visible and more convenient — to fit your habits.

Full-screen incoming call or notification

Choose how an incoming call is shown: a full-screen view with “answer” and “decline” buttons or a compact heads-up notification. In full-screen mode a contact's individual ringtone works.

Flash and edge light

Don't miss calls even when the phone is silent: the camera can flash, and a thin light frame can gently pulse along the edge of the screen. The frame color can be chosen from several options.

Answer by swipe or buttons

  • two separate “answer” and “decline” buttons;
  • a left-right or up-down swipe with a single button;
  • colored hint arrows: green to answer, red to decline;
  • pick whatever works best for you.

Auto-answer when raised to your ear

Enable proximity auto-answer: on an incoming call just raise the phone to your ear and the call is answered automatically. Handy when your hands are busy. On the lock screen you can hide the caller's name and number.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the incoming call screen

Can I turn on flash on call?

Yes. The call settings include a “Flash on call” effect: the camera flashes on an incoming call so you can see it even when the phone is silent or lying face down.

What is the edge light frame?

It is a thin line along the edge of the screen that softly pulses on an incoming call. You can choose its color from several options. It is a noticeable but non-intrusive call signal.

How do I answer a call by swiping?

The answer style is configurable: two separate buttons, a left-right swipe or an up-down swipe. You can pick what suits you and enable colored hint arrows.

Can the phone answer automatically when raised to my ear?

Yes. Enable auto-answer on raise to ear: on an incoming call the proximity sensor answers automatically when you bring the phone to your ear, after which the screen turns off as usual.

Can I hide the caller on the lock screen?

Yes. There is an option to hide the caller on the lock screen — on a locked phone the notification shows only a generic call title, without the number or name.

Why doesn't the full-screen incoming call appear on Xiaomi?

On Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO you need to grant a few permissions manually once: background pop-up windows, showing on the lock screen and full-screen notifications. A detailed guide is in the FAQ on the homepage.
